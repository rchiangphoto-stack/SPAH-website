Good to know

Questions pet parents ask us.

Grooming for pets who need a little extra care — anxious, senior, matted, or turned away elsewhere.

Do you groom anxious, nervous, or senior dogs?
Yes. Because our grooming happens inside a working animal hospital, the same team that knows your dog's history is right down the hall. We go at your dog's pace, take breaks when they need one, and keep things calm for pets who find grooming stressful or who are older and need a gentler, slower visit.
My dog was turned away by another groomer — can you help?
Often, yes. We regularly see dogs who have had a hard time at a salon, whether from fear, age, or a health condition. Having veterinarians on-site means we can groom pets that a standalone shop may not feel comfortable taking. If a dog truly can't tolerate grooming, our veterinary team will talk through the right options with you first.
Can you groom a badly matted dog or cat?
Yes. Heavy matting is uncomfortable and can hide skin problems underneath, so comfort always comes before looks — sometimes that means a shorter cut to start fresh. Because we're also a veterinary clinic, we can check the skin beneath the coat and flag anything that needs a closer look.
Is grooming at a vet different from a regular salon?
The grooming itself is familiar — baths, tidies, and full breed cuts. What's different is the setting: your pet is cared for by a team with medical oversight nearby, and you can pair a groom with a wellness visit in the same trip, which is especially helpful for anxious, senior, or health-sensitive pets.
Do you groom cats?
Cat grooming is on the way. When it launches, matted, senior, and handling-sensitive cats will be especially welcome, since the vet setting lets us keep them comfortable. Ask us to add you to the list and we'll reach out when cat grooming opens.
Where are you located and what areas do you serve?
Grooming is at South Pasadena Animal Hospital, 3116 W Main St, Alhambra, CA 91801. We welcome pet parents from Alhambra, South Pasadena, San Gabriel, San Marino, Monterey Park, and the greater Pasadena and San Gabriel Valley area. Grooming is by appointment.
